## Top Signals

- **Windows agent telemetry deserves a narrow, evidence-led explanation.** A researcher examined one ARM64VM with optional diagnostics enabled; the Hacker News thread reached686 points/552 comments, up from149/60 the previous evening. Inspect the method before generalizing to other machines. This is renewed discussion of an existing report, not a new platform-wide finding. [Research](https://xusheng.dev/posts/windows-coding-agent-telemetry/) · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50524736).
- **Measure completed work and its failure cost.** RUNTA's November 17 vendor-hosted evaluation spans22 configurations, 5 models, 62 tasks and 4 attempts per cell. Failed/timed-out trials accounted for64.5% of its estimated list-price inference cost. The narrow lesson is to include retries and acceptance checks; the experiment does not establish a universal best harness. [Study](https://runta.com/blog/introducing-frontierharness-eval-v1-1/) · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50532191).
- **Make verification match the change.** Anthropic describes owner-written checks and diff-triggered review, with end-to-end, property and differential tests serving different purposes. Its account of internal practice is useful design input, with no independent performance claim implied. [Engineering article](https://claude.dev/blog/how-the-claude-code-team-thinks-about-verification/) · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50529612).

Argon remains its default recommendation for ordinary work. Availability does not establish lower accepted-task cost. [Announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/).
- **AlphaProof Code makes the specification boundary visible.** DeepMind reports verifying key properties of Signal's compiled cryptographic code with human-reviewed specifications and Lean-checked proofs. This establishes scoped properties, rather than a guarantee that every part of Signal is invulnerable. [Research](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/).
- **Muse Ember targets repeated agent work.** Meta positions the hosted model for tool calls and coding subtasks. Count retries and review effort before treating a lower unit price as savings. Vendor economics and demos were not reproduced. [Primary](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-ember).
- **Read the review boundary before connecting data.** Google's Connected Apps policy describes human review under its applicable Personal Intelligence scope. The current discussion is new; the policy's revision date is unresolved. [Official help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16836988).

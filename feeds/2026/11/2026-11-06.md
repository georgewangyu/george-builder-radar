## Builder Takeaways

- Build: use separate observable checks for completed work, permitted actions and information disclosure in an existing workflow.
- Study: documented filter exclusions, benchmark comparison assumptions and cloud-versus-local execution boundaries.
- Ignore: reach as adoption proof, stars as retention, and hypothetical output gains as measured results.

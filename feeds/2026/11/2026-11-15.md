## Builder Takeaways

- Make permission checks a visible shipping receipt, including signed-out and cross-account cases on your own synthetic fixtures.
- Distinguish an author's argument, a vendor demonstration and an independently reproduced finding.
- Study human feedback and skill evaluation before adding orchestration. Large counters do not prove task success.
- Excluded NVIDIA motive allegations and an unresolved deletion anecdote because factual support was insufficient. Older model announcements remain context rather than fresh launch headlines.

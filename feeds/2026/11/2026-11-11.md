## Builder Takeaways

- Build: define the costly error, representative evaluation cases and rollback trigger before a model swap.
- Study: trace a shared result back to its query and access boundary; trace a discovery back to prior literature.
- Ignore: claims that views establish adoption, agreement establishes correctness, or a task-specific saving proves general model superiority.

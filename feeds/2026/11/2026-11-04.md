## Builder Takeaways

- Build: make instruction authority and approval transitions explicit and reviewable.
- Study: connect actual released artifacts, licensing, cost and independent checks before calling a model or workflow usable.
- Ignore: unconfirmed model-release dates and data-theft allegations without mechanism-level evidence.

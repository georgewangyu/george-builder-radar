## Builder Takeaways

- Build: representative acceptance tests and an uncertainty fallback before expanding autonomy.
- Study: the full announcement and access/retirement terms, then test one real task.
- Ignore: speculative motives, unsupported founder adoption totals and treating a demo as production evidence.

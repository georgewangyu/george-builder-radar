## Late-Breaking AI Signal — 17:10 PDT

- **[Agent Plugins 1.0](https://agent-plugins.org/) creates a portable floor
  for skills plus MCP servers across major agent clients.**
  - Signal: the [OpenAI Developers launch](https://x.com/OpenAIDevs/status/2085398373511918022)
    reached `960,854 views` in about `9h`, roughly `2.47x` the account's
    current followers. Amazon, Cursor, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Vercel are
    represented among the initial core maintainers; HN discussion remained
    shallow.
  - Why it matters: package one small skill-plus-MCP plugin once and test it in
    two clients. The real story is which discovery, permission, secret,
    installation, update, and hook semantics are actually portable.

- **[Cloudflare Kitesurf](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kitesurf/) is a
  purpose-built browser runtime for agents.**
  - Signal: the [Cloudflare Developers launch](https://x.com/CloudflareDev/status/2085394318005846411)
    reached `308,538 views`, roughly `4.07x` the account's current followers.
    Cloudflare reports `3-7x` lower CPU/memory use than Chromium for measured
    tasks; the direct HN thread was only `18 / 3`.
  - Why it matters: compare one screenshot/HTML-extraction workload against
    Chromium and preserve resource use, browser fidelity, network boundaries,
    state, and teardown. Treat vendor benchmarks as hypotheses.

- **[GPT-5.6 Sol changed in ChatGPT while Work and Codex did not](https://openai.com/index/improving-gpt-5-6-sol-in-chatgpt/).**
  - Signal: the [official post](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2085434712429052386)
    reached `1,164,800 views` in about `5.6h`; the [HN thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49199357)
    reached `133 / 90`. OpenAI's `68%` factual-error reduction is an internal
    evaluation, not independent proof.
  - Why it matters: rerun a fixed factual prompt set before and after the
    change, preserving citations, corrections, response length, and reasoning
    setting. Do not imply the Codex or Work model changed.

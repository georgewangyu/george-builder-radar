## Builder Takeaways

- Build: make one delegated workflow inspectable with scoped permissions, a safe exit, an append-only receipt, and a human-readable diagram.
- Study: Archify for portable visuals, Maka for event history, Caddi for exception handling, and Microduck for a physical training-loop proof.
- Ignore: acquisition certainty without a company primary, stars or views presented as adoption, and generic agent wrappers without a permission or evidence model.

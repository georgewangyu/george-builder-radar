## Builder Takeaways

- **Build:** a claim-to-certificate worksheet or an assertion-versus-action
  permission matrix—one small proof object, not a new platform.
- **Study:** `qm` room scope and audit behavior; Seedance accepted-output cost;
  Zinley's identity and truth boundaries.
- **Ignore:** unverified model codenames, raw star totals as adoption proof,
  and any benchmark that omits the failed-attempt or accepted-task denominator.

## Builder Takeaways

- Build: one fixed-task receipt for a tiny agent or one reproducible Apple-Silicon inference comparison.
- Study: PR comprehension gates, high-quality diagram skills, MCP token overhead, and reasoning-trace retention boundaries.
- Ignore: generic “local AI” demos without task success, lifetime stars presented as adoption, and resurfaced launch pages presented as fresh announcements.

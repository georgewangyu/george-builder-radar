## Late-Breaking AI Signal

- Signal: [OpenAI and Cursor's model-access dispute kept accelerating](https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex/)
  - Point-in-time evidence: Cursor CEO Michael Truell's [response](https://x.com/mntruell/status/2093532254006063557) rose from the preserved August 29 receipt of `3,049,454` views to `5,774,808` views, or `25.84x` his current follower count.   - Why it matters: Archify added `14,875` stars on weekly Trending while Apache Maka records messages, calls, permissions, and termination in append-only history. The useful product boundary is becoming reviewable execution, not another opaque orchestration layer.
  - Source: [Archify](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify), [Apache Maka](https://github.com/apache/maka)
- Signal: [Anthropic previewed the Model Hardware Standard](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview)
  - Why it matters: physical-agent interfaces need explicit commands, interlocks, approvals, error recovery, deterministic real-time boundaries, and a reliable stop mechanism. This is a research preview; Anthropic says open sourcing will come later.
  - Source: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview)
- Signal: [An older Claude Code session-URL default dispute resurfaced](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/66504)
  - Why it matters: the June 9 issue was closed on August 17 after Anthropic documented that the URL appears only for web sessions or Remote Control and can be disabled with `attribution.sessionUrl: false`. The discussion later reached Hacker News at `177` points / `201` comments, but neither source establishes public session access, a new disclosure, or affected scope.
  - Source: [GitHub issue](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/66504), [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49498201)

## Builder Takeaways

- Build: one synthetic `agent-run-receipt` with inputs, permissions, retries, checkpoints, evaluator, termination, and accepted output.
- Study: Archify for portable proof, Maka for append-only history, and Scientific Agent Skills for catalog validation and provenance.
- Ignore: capacity percentages without baselines, security conclusions from one user report, and stars/views presented as adoption.

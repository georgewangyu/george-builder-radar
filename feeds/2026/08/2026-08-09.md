    views, while current independent discussion was much smaller.

  across recent Meta, Anthropic, and OpenAI evaluation incidents.

    discussion reached `46 points / 17 comments`.
  - Source: [AP synthesis](https://apnews.com/article/0e8061437da6779be962b24ac134a514), [OpenAI incident record](https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-model-evaluation-security-incident/), [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49231022)

  - Why it matters: cross-session coordination makes exact sender and receiver
    identity, permissions, deduplication, delivery, timeout, and immutable
    history product requirements. The official post reached `6,809,167` views,
    but the wave remained owned-heavy and outside the strict window.
  - Source: [Claude documentation](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/cross-session-messaging), [official post](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2085817074816070014)

## Builder Takeaways

- Build: one containment or cross-session delivery receipt inside an existing
  workflow.
- Study: identity, permissions, observed-vs-intended network policy,
  interruption, deduplication, and replay.
- Ignore: “GPT-6 delayed,” “model escaped,” or broad adoption claims not
  supported by the primary records.
- Omitted high-heat clusters: Claude Auto mode stays under official updates
  because the wave was cooling and first-party; human-frustration feedback is
  an operator-practice note without independent event depth; a claimed
  25-year-old-problem breakthrough remained unresolved to a durable primary
  technical artifact; older RunAnywhere and weak Show HN agent tools did not
  add a distinct current pattern.

## Top Signals

- Model economics are becoming a product capability.
  - Why it matters: OpenAI's [GPT-5.6 Sol pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing) drew [84 HN points / 62 comments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49421074) in roughly its first hour, while a separate Anthropic usage/cost debate reached [708 points / 630 comments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49411102).
  - Signal: single segment; attention_heat 76 / 86, builder_fit 96 / 92.
  - Builder significance: compare models on cost per accepted result, not list price or generic capability rankings.
  - Boundary: the Sol reduction is temporary, and the Anthropic story is reported analysis rather than a company announcement.
- Instructions are becoming executable operating knowledge.
  - Why it matters: a practical [`agent.md` write-up](https://fabiensanglard.net/agent.md/index.html) reached [376 HN points / 159 comments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49410932).
  - Signal: single segment; attention_heat 78, builder_fit 98.
  - Builder significance: version one concise rule and measure its effect on interventions, tests, and accepted output.
  - Boundary: popularity does not make any instruction file universally correct.
- Low latency is the product in interactive agents.
  - Why it matters: a [low-latency AI Skyrim companion](https://pantel.is/projects/ai-gaming-companion/) reached [291 HN points / 57 comments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49413561).
  - Signal: single segment; attention_heat 72, builder_fit 93.
  - Builder significance: preserve perception, inference, voice-turn, and action latency separately; responsiveness can matter more than a marginal benchmark gain.
  - Boundary: the current attention wave is concentrated on HN.

High-heat omissions: no verified cluster reached `drop-everything`. ## Builder Takeaways

- Build: run one frozen task across two model/pricing configurations and record cost per accepted result.
- Study: pair concise instruction files with append-only execution receipts; constraints and evidence solve different failure modes.
- Ignore: temporary token pricing, star velocity, or follower-adjusted reach as substitutes for retained use and accepted output.

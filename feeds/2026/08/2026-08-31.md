## Late-Breaking AI Signal

- Signal: [ChatGPT Ads reached a `$1B` annualized revenue run rate in fewer than `200` days](https://openai.com/index/expanding-access-to-ai-with-chatgpt-ads/)
  - Point-in-time evidence: OpenAI says the platform has tens of thousands of advertisers, operates in more than `40` countries, is opening self-service access across India, Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa, and now has CPC and outcome-optimized bidding on a majority of campaigns. Independent same-day coverage included [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/openais-ad-business-hits-1-billion-annualized-revenue-run-rate-4882977) and [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/31/openai-chatgpt-ads-1b-revenue-run-rate).
  - Why builders should care: conversational products are becoming measurable decision surfaces. Preserve a hard boundary between answers and sponsored inventory, make targeting controls legible, and leave attribution and conversion receipts that can be audited.
  - Caveat: annualized run rate extrapolates a current pace; it is not `$1B` already collected over a year, and the announcement does not provide an independent profit, advertiser-retention, or representative ROAS series.

## GitHub / Repo Radar

- [yc-software/qm](https://github.com/yc-software/qm)
  - What it does: multiplayer agent harness with personal and shared scopes.
  - Why builders should care: inspect credentials, approvals, destructive-
    command boundaries, and audit behavior before copying the interface.
- [kvcache-ai/AgentENV](https://github.com/kvcache-ai/AgentENV)
  - What it does: runs distributed agent environments at scale.
  - Why builders should care: reproducibility and accepted-task reliability
    matter more than raw environment throughput.
- [trycompai/crm](https://github.com/trycompai/crm)
  - What it does: open-source, agentic-first CRM.
  - Why builders should care: a concrete revenue workflow is a clearer wedge
    than another general-purpose agent framework.
- [QwenAudio/qwen-audio-agent](https://github.com/QwenAudio/qwen-audio-agent)
  - What it does: real-time voice runtime for agents.
  - Why builders should care: evaluate latency, barge-in, interruption, and
    tool-state continuity rather than the voice demo alone.
- [QoderAI/better-harness](https://github.com/QoderAI/better-harness)
  - What it does: turns session evidence into verifiable loop-level next steps.
  - Why builders should care: evidence-backed handoffs are a sharper product
    surface than generic “agent memory.”

If confirmed, model selection, usage metering, billing, and switching costs become one strategic infrastructure layer.

  - Source: [Polar's July 29 primary launch](https://polarbrowser.com/blog/introducing-polar)
- **Yap on-device dictation — `single segment`**
  - Why it matters: Show HN exposed `96` points / `38` comments at about `22h`. Local, open-source dictation with no model download is a clean product wedge and a useful privacy/latency comparison surface.
  - Source: [Hacker News Show](https://news.ycombinator.com/show)

## Builder Takeaways

- Build: a containment review that follows the full dependency chain—sandbox,
  package proxy, credentials, egress, compute budget, stop conditions,
  monitoring, and third-party boundaries.
- Study: practical model routing and recurring-agent regression. Record the
  predicted route, actual success, misroutes, retries, intervention, latency,
  and completed-task cost.
- Ignore: oracle benchmark ceilings, launch-board placement, star spikes, and
  cumulative views as substitutes for reproducible task evidence or durable
  adoption.

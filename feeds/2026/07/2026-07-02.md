- Signal: Agent products are moving from chat windows into action surfaces.
  - Why it matters: The useful frontier is permissioned execution, resumability, audit trails, and domain-native interfaces.
  - Source: Product Hunt launches for Acti, Tabstack, Sequence, Airtop; Launch HN Intuned/Runtime/Superset.

- Signal: Agent workflows are being packaged as named skills, frameworks, and role packs.
  - Why it matters: The market is rewarding repeatable workflows more than one-off prompts.
  - Source: GitHub trending repos `obra/superpowers`, `agentskills/agentskills`, `msitarzewski/agency-agents`, `JuliusBrussee/caveman`.

- Signal: Creator and developer tooling are converging.
  - Why it matters: Coding agents are increasingly used to produce video, research, browser automations, and public artifacts.
  - Source: `calesthio/OpenMontage`, `browser-use/video-use`, Fuser, Tabstack, Airtop.

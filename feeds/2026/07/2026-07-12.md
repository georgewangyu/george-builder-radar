    and readable denial receipts.
  - Source: https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/destructive_command_guard

    the real product surface for asynchronous work.
  - Source: https://github.com/ColeMurray/background-agents

- Repo: [`TencentCloud/CubeSandbox`](https://github.com/TencentCloud/CubeSandbox)
  - What it does: supplies sandbox and runtime infrastructure for agent work.
  - Why builders should care: untrusted workloads need explicit isolation and
    lifecycle contracts.
  - Source: https://github.com/TencentCloud/CubeSandbox

- Repo: [`PrefectHQ/prefect`](https://github.com/PrefectHQ/prefect)

## Builder Takeaways

- Build: an agent-egress receipt that records external destinations, file
  classes, and payload categories for a fixed coding task.
- Study: portable task contracts across Codex, Claude Code, and Grok Build;
  include budgets, acceptance tests, intervention points, and final receipts.
- Ignore: rumors without canonical announcements, cumulative popularity
  counters presented as current velocity, and launch reach presented as proof
  of adoption, retention, or revenue.

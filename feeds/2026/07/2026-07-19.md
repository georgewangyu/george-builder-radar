  - Freshness: Qwen's canonical post reached **2.76M views / 20.5K
    engagements** in about 7.6 hours, while the direct
    [Hacker News discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48966120)

  - Caveat: Qwen had not released the weights, model card, final license,

  operations story (**attention heat 87 / builder fit 89**).
  - Freshness: Moonshot's new capacity post reached **406.5K views / 8,376
    engagements** in roughly 1.2 hours. The broader
    [K3 launch](https://x.com/Kimi_Moonshot/status/2077830229968683203)
    reached **22.3M views**, and fresh
    [Hacker News discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48960218)
    reached **553 points / 519 comments**.

    queueing, and whether a paid user can reliably finish the task. A capacity
    pause is direct operational evidence, not proof of retention or paid
    adoption.
  - Primary source: [Moonshot's capacity announcement](https://x.com/Kimi_Moonshot/status/2078855608565207130).

- Signal: GPT-5.6's Lean-verified convex-optimization result is the strongest
  proof-backed research cluster (**attention heat 77 / builder fit 89**,
  **single segment**).
  - Freshness: the author reports that a **148-minute** model session, guided
    by a roughly ten-page expert prompt, produced the main argument for a lower
    bound open since 1996; the author checked it and verified it in Lean. The
    fresh [Hacker News thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48957779)
    reached **576 points / 371 comments**.
  - Builder significance: the useful artifact is the whole research receipt—
    expert framing, prior attempts, model session, human checking, formal
    verification, and peer-review status—not “AI solved math alone.”
  - Caveat: the preprint is public but not yet peer reviewed.
  - Primary source: [the public preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13335).

- Signal: Claude Code weekly limits remain 50% higher through August 19
  (**attention heat 75 / builder fit 87**, **single segment**).
  - Freshness: the canonical post reached **2.38M views / 21.4K engagements**
    after about 24 hours, with
    [independent Hacker News discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48961157)
    at **66 points / 70 comments**.
  - Builder significance: compare completed-task value per subscription
    dollar across resets, higher limits, and split plans rather than treating
    a larger quota as a capability improvement.
  - Primary source: [Claude Developers' canonical post](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2078511173759324328).

## Builder Takeaways

- Build: one provider-neutral complete-task receipt across Qwen 3.8 Preview,
  Kimi K3, Claude, and GPT-5.6: availability, limits, effective context,
  latency, cost, interventions, decisions, recovery, and final quality.
- Study: agent decision audits, persistent code maps, and trace-driven browser
  reliability as proof-oriented control surfaces.
- Ignore: “open soon,” model rank, quota size, star spikes, and launch-board
  placement as substitutes for downloadable artifacts and reproducible task
  evidence.

## Builder Takeaways

- Build: a research-receipt verifier that records the expert question,
  prompt/session, primary claim, executable or symbolic check, independent
  reviewers, and publication state.
- Study: mutable-model benchmark receipts and agent control surfaces—replay,
  inbox, identity, deployment, context, and failure capture.
- Ignore: model ranks, launch-board placement, star spikes, and cumulative
  views as substitutes for reproducible task evidence or durable adoption.

## Top Signals

- Signal: [OpenAI's Work/Codex usage, credits, and capacity
  wave](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2077271889626706300) is hot at
  **89 attention heat / 97 builder fit**.
  - Evidence: the official near-9M update reached 1.15M views; a
    [$100-credit promotion](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2077248807533003257)
    reached 2.85M views, and [Sam Altman said Sol growth was straining inference
    capacity](https://x.com/sama/status/2077106587307798989). Independent Reddit
    threads surfaced capacity complaints and disputed what the count means
    after an app merger.
  - Why it matters: migration, credits, repeated resets, and active-user
    definitions can make distribution look like durable adoption. Builders
    should measure completed tasks, retries, review cost, and steady-state use.
  - Publishing move: **single segment**. Audit the denominator and cost per
    verified task instead of repeating the 9M headline.

- Signal: [Demis Hassabis's frontier AI standards
  proposal](https://x.com/demishassabis/status/2076957440109625718) is hot at
  **91 attention heat / 78 builder fit**.
  - Evidence: the primary reached 8.88M views and spread through responses from
    leaders at OpenAI, Microsoft, Google, and xAI, alongside an
    [Axios interview](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/14/demis-hassabis-ai-regulation-google-deepmind)
    and Hacker News discussion.
  - Why it matters: shared testing thresholds and release gates could become
    operating constraints for frontier-model builders, but this remains an
    industry proposal rather than enacted policy.
  - Publishing move: **single segment by editorial override**. Explain who sets
    thresholds, what triggers a test or slowdown, and how open models fit.

- Signal: [PrismML's Bonsai 27B](https://prismml.com/news/bonsai-27b) is hot at
  **86 attention heat / 94 builder fit**.
  - Evidence: the [launch post](https://x.com/PrismML/status/2077084891284721827)
    reached 1.07M views from 14,793 followers, while Hacker News reached
    [374 points / 144 comments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48910545).
    Apache-2.0 weights include 5.9 GB ternary and 3.9 GB 1-bit variants.
  - Why it matters: a downloadable 27B-class model on a phone is a falsifiable
    builder story. The reported capability retention remains a vendor benchmark
    until reproduced on real hardware.
  - Publishing move: **single segment**. Test latency, quality, thermals,
    battery use, and tool reliability on one fixed task.

- Signal: [the Grok Build upload and deletion-response
  wave](https://x.com/hrkrshnn/status/2076716354754015368) is peaking at
  **84 attention heat / 99 builder fit**.
  - Evidence: the technical primary reached 3.21M views from 12,228 followers;
    Hacker News reached [533 points / 228 comments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48877371),
    and [SpaceXAI's response](https://x.com/SpaceXAI/status/2076692402442846289)
    reached 4.51M views.
  - Why it matters: deletion is not a trust feature without a user-verifiable
    receipt. Local logs can show collection and egress, but cannot prove every
    remote retention, backup, or derived-data state.
  - Publishing move: **single segment**. Run the read-only audit and clearly
    separate upload, recognition, retention, and deletion claims.

- Signal: [a camera-roll wardrobe
  workflow](https://x.com/cdngdev/status/2076812846793650485) is peaking at
  **80 attention heat / 94 builder fit**.
  - Evidence: the demo reached 5.15M views from 6,758 followers, an exceptional
    account-relative breakout, though independent non-X discussion remained thin.
  - Why it matters: capability can compress a startup into a workflow, while
    permissions, taxonomy, repeatability, and deletion remain product layers.
  - Publishing move: **single segment**. Teardown the pipeline and its trust
    envelope rather than treating a spectacular demo as a finished product.

- Signal: [Claude for Teachers](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2077047278078931243)
  is hot at **78 attention heat / 81 builder fit**.
  - Evidence: the official post reached 5.50M views. The offer combines premium
    access, teaching skills, standards-mapped curricula, educator verification,
    and a FERPA-oriented data agreement.
  - Why it matters: vertical AI packaging is model access plus workflows,
    domain content, privacy terms, onboarding, and institutional distribution.
  - Publishing move: **single segment** on vertical packaging, not a generic
    “AI in schools” story.

- Coverage note: **no verified drop-everything event and no scored high-heat
  cluster was omitted**. Kimi K3 remained rumor-only without a Moonshot/Kimi
  primary; the reported Claude Opus 5 Vertex AI item lacked an official
  Anthropic or Google announcement; Meta's physics-olympiad result and
  Perplexity WANDR did not show enough independent spread at cutoff.

## Builder Takeaways

- Build: a phone-model reproduction harness that records file size, device,
  latency, thermals, battery, task quality, tool use, and failures.
- Measure: agent adoption with explicit denominators and cost per completed
  task, including retries, review effort, and promotional credits.
- Study: `destructive_command_guard` plus CubeSandbox as complementary guard
  and isolation layers; OfficeCLI and Tiptap for structured document edits.
- Steal: the narrow-skill-plus-fresh-data pattern from Crustdata and the
  shared-editable-artifact pattern across Campus, Tiptap, and YC launches.
- Ignore: cumulative views, stars, launch votes, vendor benchmarks, or user
  counts presented as proof of adoption, retention, reliability, or revenue.

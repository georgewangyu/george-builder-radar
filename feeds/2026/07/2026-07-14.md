## Builder Takeaways

- Build: an agent-egress canary that records file access, destinations,
  retention controls, and recovery receipts.
- Study: the wardrobe workflow as a six-stage pipeline; `destructive_command_guard`
  and CubeSandbox as complementary containment patterns.
- Measure: completed-task cost across tokens, elapsed time, retries, tool calls,
  reviewer corrections, and output quality.
- Ignore: cumulative stars, launch votes, executive usage claims, or one user
  report presented as proof of adoption, revenue, retention, or universal behavior.

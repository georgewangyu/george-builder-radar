## Builder Takeaways

- Build: an accepted-output receipt for an existing task, including calls, retries, output and time. A shorter trace matters only if quality survives.
- Study: feedback mechanisms in agent-ready codebases, mixed model benchmark rows, and skill-scanner detection boundaries.
- Ignore: stars as usage proof, a proposedIPO as a completed listing, secondary financial figures without inspected disclosures, and a preview as working access.

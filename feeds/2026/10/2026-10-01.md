## Builder Takeaways

- **Build:** on one existing task, record an accepted artifact, checkpoint and stopping rule before expanding output or authority.
- **Study:** compare hardware-specific inference at equal quality, and inspect memory/permission contracts directly.
- **Ignore:** lifetime star counts as adoption, retweet-inherited breakout ratios, funding as technical validation, and announcement as general availability.

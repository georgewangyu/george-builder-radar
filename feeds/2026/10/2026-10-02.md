## Builder Takeaways

- **Build:** on an existing synthetic task, record interrupted operation identity, observed outcome and the rule for replay or escalation.
- **Study:** compare decision probabilities with the application's permission policy, and partial speech hypotheses with finalized input.
- **Ignore:** session counts as efficiency, lifetime stars as adoption, vendor latency as a reproduced local result, and social reach as canonical event heat.

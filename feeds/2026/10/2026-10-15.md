## Builder Takeaways

- Build acceptance evidence into an existing workflow before adding concurrency.
- Study version aliases, context persistence, tenant isolation and review decisions as separate contracts.
- Ignore untested adoption claims, cumulative-star races and automatic universal-best-model conclusions.

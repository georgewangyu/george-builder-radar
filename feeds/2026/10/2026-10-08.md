## Builder Takeaways

- Build: an accepted-task receipt with prompt length, retries, latency, review time and pass/fail.
- Study: version and dependency provenance; inspect generated interfaces by changing one input.
- Ignore: cumulative stars as usage, a high-view reaction as verified policy, a preorder as performance proof, or funding as retention.

## Builder Takeaways

- Build: record model version, harness version, full task cost and human-accepted outcome together before judging a cheaper model.
- Study: explicit evaluation boundaries, memory handoffs and context compression before adding another orchestration layer.
- Ignore: conflating promised weights with released weights, pledged equity with delivered aid, or cumulative views and repository stars with adoption.

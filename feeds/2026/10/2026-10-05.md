## Builder Takeaways

- Build proof around accepted task quality, complete resource requirements and failure behavior.
- Study promises separately from shipped changes and account terms.
- Treat watermarking as limited provenance and ad attribution as distinct from causal lift.
- Ignore release recaps presented as new events and stars presented as adoption.

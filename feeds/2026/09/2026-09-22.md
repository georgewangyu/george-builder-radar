## Top Signals

- Signal: [Grok 4.7](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7) reached `592` Hacker News
  points / `499` comments in about 23 hours.
  - Why it matters: the release makes a concrete price/performance claim for
    multi-hour coding work, but the published benchmark table is vendor
    evidence.
  - Action: **single segment** — run one fixed task against a known-good model
    and preserve price, latency, interventions, tests, and accepted output.
- Signal: [Clueso MCP](https://www.producthunt.com/products/clueso) ranked
  Product Hunt `#1` with `326` points and `48` launch comments at snapshot.
  - Why it matters: granular timeline tools, multi-frame inspection,
    deterministic edit feedback, and a human-editable result are a stronger
    agent-video pattern than one-shot generation.
  - Action: **single segment** — test one bounded real edit and inspect the
    feedback/reversibility loop.
- Signal: [Google AX](https://github.com/google/ax) showed `7,161★`, `338`
  forks, and `+2,324` stars on daily Trending.
  - Why it matters: execution state, resumption, and observable control are
    becoming distinct builder surfaces above model capability.
  - Action: **single segment** — study the architecture before considering any
    stack change.

## Builder Takeaways

- Build: one accepted-work receipt for a consequential agent task: inputs, permissions, actions, reversibility, cost, elapsed time, tests, interventions, failures, and final acceptance.
- Preserve: a Hugging Face portability baseline now, before product and distribution behavior can change.
- Test: one K2 checkpoint from checksum to accepted output; separate artifact availability from runtime reproducibility.
- Study: skill packaging and proof artifacts selectively. Do not install broad agent frameworks because their repositories are trending.
- Omitted from promotion: PAIR and Grok Bot have strong builder fit but only owned-release evidence and no substantial independent discussion; Gemini 3.8 and Meta Muse remain active but their primary launches are outside this 24-hour window and their action levels did not change.

## Builder Takeaways

- Build: Improve one existing instruction file and measure correction rate, review edits, tests, and acceptance on a frozen task.
- Study: Compare skill repositories and verifiable diagram tools for structure, validation, and handoff quality.
- Ignore: Raw stars, views, and vendor cost claims as evidence of adoption, retention, safety, or production success.

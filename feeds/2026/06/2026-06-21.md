# George's Builder Radar - 2026-06-21

Updated when George ran his morning routine on 2026-06-21.

## Top Signals

- Signal: Agent work is moving from chat demos into durable work surfaces:
  skills, memory, sandboxes, app/browser control, review loops, and receipts.
  - Why it matters: the useful builder question is no longer "can an agent
    answer?" It is "what real surface can it touch, what proof does it leave,
    and when should it stop?"
  - Source: X, GitHub trending, Product Hunt, and Launch YC / HN.
- Signal: Markdown is becoming a practical agent packaging format.
  - Why it matters: a folder of instructions, examples, scripts, and source
    receipts is becoming a product surface that can be cloned, reviewed, and
    improved.
  - Source: https://x.com/rauchg/status/2068165988005380478
- Signal: The strongest public examples are receipt-heavy, not hype-heavy.
  - Why it matters: builders should bias toward visible artifacts: traces,
    repos, screenshots, demos, before/after states, and failure cases.
  - Source: https://x.com/mitsuhiko/status/2068657029443363136

  condition, artifact output, and one visible failure case.

    act, verify, and stop.
  - Source: https://x.com/shmidtqq/status/2071256171428630687
- Signal: Tiny AI-first teams are becoming an executive story, not only a
  developer story.
  - Why it matters: The market wants operating models for small teams using
    coding agents, not just tools.
  - Source: https://x.com/clairevo/status/2071619880043098588
- Signal: Product Hunt and Launch HN both showed narrow, artifact-specific AI
  tools: email tabs, receipt extraction, testing agents, CAD, architecture, and
  agent analytics.
  - Why it matters: Specific inputs and outputs beat broad "AI workspace"
    promises.
  - Source: https://www.producthunt.com/

- Signal: Agent tools are moving from chat/code generation into operational surfaces: MCP apps, evals, memory, skill security, browser automation, and media production.
  - Why it matters: Builders need proof loops, review surfaces, and receipts, not just model access.
  - Source: Product Hunt daily leaderboard, GitHub trending, Launch HN, X timeline.

- Signal: "MCP apps are the new website" is today's sharpest product frame.
  - Why it matters: It points to a new UI target: interactive app surfaces inside assistants.
  - Source: Skybridge Product Hunt launch and Paul Graham's "AI apps are the new browsers" post.

- Signal: Agent loop interest is colliding with fear of slop.
  - Why it matters: The winning workflow products will help users choose the right loop, verify output, and keep review/audit trails.
  - Source: Peter Yang, Matt Van Horn, AgentX, Kepler, and Codex side-chat feedback.
